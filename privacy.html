WatsBuy Privacy Policy

📅 Effective date: 26 October 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

WatsBuy is a commerce technology platform that helps businesses create and manage digital storefronts and product catalogues, receive and manage orders, communicate with customers through supported messaging channels, run permitted campaigns, process or link to payments, and use automation, analytics and AI-assisted tools.

WatsBuy is operated by Ziro Systems Private Limited ("Ziro", "WatsBuy", "we", "us" or "our"). Registered/business address: Alappat Buildings, No. VII/27-E, Muthuvara, Puzhakkal (P.O), Thrissur, Kerala – 680553, India. Website: https://watsbuy.com.

This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal data when you visit our website, create or administer a merchant account, use WatsBuy tools, interact with a WatsBuy-powered store, communicate with a merchant through a supported channel, contact us, or otherwise interact with our services.

This Policy does not replace a merchant's own privacy notice. Merchants using WatsBuy may independently decide why and how they use shopper information. Their own notices and terms govern their store, products, fulfilment, returns and marketing practices.

2. Roles: WatsBuy, Merchants and Shoppers

Depending on the activity and applicable law, WatsBuy may act as a data fiduciary/controller for information used to operate our business, manage merchant accounts, provide support, secure the platform, bill subscriptions and improve our services. When we process shopper personal data on a merchant's behalf to provide storefront, order-management, messaging or related functionality, we generally act as a processor/service provider following the merchant's documented instructions.

The merchant is generally responsible for the products and offers it sells, the accuracy of its catalogue and policies, the lawful basis for collecting shopper data, required notices and permissions, marketing choices, order fulfilment, refunds, and responding to shopper requests concerning the merchant's use of data. If your question concerns a purchase or a message from a specific merchant, contact that merchant first. We will assist or forward requests where appropriate and legally required.

We do not own merchants' customer relationships merely because a merchant uses WatsBuy. Our processing roles may differ by feature, contract and jurisdiction.

3. Personal Data We May Collect

3.1 Information you provide

Account and business information: name, business name, job title, email address, telephone number, business address, login details, account preferences and verification information.

Store and catalogue information: product names, descriptions, images, prices, variants, stock, promotions, policies, store settings and related content uploaded by a merchant.

Order and customer information: shopper name, phone number, email, delivery/billing address, items ordered, order status, delivery instructions, support requests, returns, complaints, feedback and transaction references.

Communications: messages, requests, support tickets, feedback, campaign content, and records needed to manage customer service or business communications.

Payment and billing information: subscription plan, invoices, billing contact, payment status and transaction references. Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers; we do not intend to store full payment-card credentials unless a specific feature expressly states otherwise.

Information you submit in forms, surveys, demonstrations, onboarding, sales discussions or events.

3.2 Information collected automatically

Device, browser, operating system, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, log events, timestamps, referral pages and usage activity.

Platform performance, diagnostics, error logs, security events, feature usage and aggregate analytics.

Cookie or similar technology identifiers, where used and permitted by applicable law and your choices.

3.3 Information from integrations and third parties

We may receive information from merchants, authorised integration partners, messaging platforms, payment gateways, hosting and analytics providers, fraud-prevention providers, and other services that a merchant or user chooses to connect. The information received depends on the integration, its permissions and the applicable provider's terms.

3.4 Sensitive or high-risk information

Please do not upload government identifiers, passwords, payment-card security codes, health information or other sensitive personal data unless the relevant feature expressly requires it and the collection is lawful. Merchants must not use WatsBuy to collect or send sensitive information unlawfully.

4. How We Use Personal Data

Create, authenticate and administer merchant accounts; verify business details; configure stores and integrations.

Display product catalogues and storefronts, facilitate carts and orders, send order confirmations and status updates, and support customer service.

Enable messaging, approved templates, customer segmentation, broadcasts, abandoned-cart journeys and other merchant-directed communications.

Provide AI-assisted product discovery, conversational assistance, automation, analytics and workflow tools.

Process subscriptions, issue invoices, reconcile payments and provide billing support.

Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve WatsBuy; monitor abuse, fraud, unauthorised access and policy violations.

Respond to enquiries, provide onboarding and technical support, and communicate service notices.

Send product updates or marketing communications where permitted by law and, where required, with consent or an opt-out mechanism.

Meet legal, tax, accounting, regulatory and law-enforcement obligations; establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We seek to use personal data for specified and legitimate purposes, limit collection to what is reasonably necessary, and retain it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law.

5. Legal Bases, Consent and Choices

Depending on the jurisdiction and activity, we process personal data on the basis of consent, performance of a contract or steps requested before entering a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and other lawful grounds such as legitimate interests where recognised by applicable law. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it, subject to legal and contractual limitations and without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

Where a notice, consent, permission or opt-out is required for marketing or messaging, the responsible merchant or WatsBuy (as applicable) must obtain and maintain it. Merchants must contact people only in compliance with applicable law and relevant channel policies, honour opt-outs and suppression requests, and avoid misleading or unsolicited messages.

6. WhatsApp and Other Messaging Channels

WatsBuy may connect merchant workflows to WhatsApp or other third-party messaging channels. The availability and operation of those channels are controlled in part by their providers and may be subject to separate terms, privacy notices, fees, template approvals, quality limits and technical requirements.

Merchants are responsible for ensuring they have the required permissions and lawful basis to message each recipient, including for marketing messages and calls where applicable.

Merchants must accurately identify themselves, use approved message templates when required, respect opt-outs and blocks, and comply with WhatsApp/Meta policies and local law.

WatsBuy may process message metadata and content to deliver the requested workflow, provide support, prevent abuse, troubleshoot and maintain service security.

Messages may be processed by the relevant messaging provider and other authorised service providers under their own terms and privacy practices.

Do not assume that a message is delivered, read, approved or retained for a particular period unless the relevant platform or WatsBuy feature confirms it.

WatsBuy is an independent technology provider and is not WhatsApp or Meta. References to third-party platforms do not imply endorsement or ownership.

7. AI, Automation and Analytics

Some WatsBuy features may use AI or machine-learning services to assist with product recommendations, natural-language conversations, order-related questions, message drafting, classification, analytics or workflow automation. Inputs needed to provide a feature may be sent to authorised AI or infrastructure providers.

AI outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for a particular situation and should be reviewed where appropriate. Merchants remain responsible for their product claims, pricing, customer commitments and final business decisions. Do not submit information to an AI feature that you are not authorised to disclose.

We will not represent that personal data is never used by an AI provider for model training unless this has been verified in the applicable provider contract and configuration. We will document relevant provider practices and apply appropriate contractual and technical safeguards where available.

8. How We Share Personal Data

We may disclose personal data, limited to what is reasonably necessary, to:

The merchant whose store or service you use, and the merchant's authorised staff or service providers.

Hosting, cloud storage, security, analytics, communications, customer-support, AI, payment, invoicing and technical service providers supporting WatsBuy.

Messaging and integration providers selected to enable the requested service.

Professional advisers, auditors, insurers and corporate transaction counterparties where appropriate and subject to confidentiality safeguards.

Government authorities, regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where required by law, valid legal process, or necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

Other parties where you direct us to share information or provide valid consent.

We do not sell personal data for money. If applicable law defines certain advertising, analytics or data-sharing practices as a 'sale' or 'sharing', WatsBuy will provide the rights and choices required by that law where applicable. We do not disclose personal data to third parties for their independent marketing without a lawful basis and any required consent.

9. International Data Transfers

WatsBuy serves businesses and users in India and internationally. Personal data may be processed in India or other countries where WatsBuy and its authorised service providers operate. Those countries may have data-protection laws different from the laws where you live.

Where required, we will use appropriate transfer mechanisms and safeguards, such as contractual protections, adequacy decisions or other mechanisms recognised by applicable law. We will also comply with restrictions on cross-border transfers that apply to the relevant data and jurisdiction. Contact us for further information about relevant safeguards, subject to security and confidentiality restrictions.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data for the period reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain business and security records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal, tax or accounting requirements. Retention periods vary according to the type of data, the feature used, merchant instructions and applicable law.

When a merchant account is closed or a merchant requests deletion, we will take steps to delete or de-identify data we control when appropriate, subject to lawful retention requirements, backups, security logs, dispute preservation and technical limitations. Data held by a merchant or a third-party provider may need to be deleted by that merchant or provider. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten under normal backup cycles.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Measures may include access controls, authentication, monitoring, backups, secure development practices and vendor controls appropriate to the service and risk.

No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users and merchants must protect credentials, restrict staff access, promptly report suspected compromise and avoid sharing passwords or unnecessary personal data. We will handle security incidents and provide notices to affected parties or authorities when required by applicable law.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or platform may use essential cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, protect accounts, remember preferences, understand site performance and, where enabled, measure marketing effectiveness. You can control cookies through your browser and any consent tool we provide. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality. If WatsBuy uses non-essential analytics or advertising cookies, the website should provide any consent and preference controls required by the laws applicable to visitors.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Your rights depend on where you live, the applicable law, and whether WatsBuy or a merchant controls the relevant processing. Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, rights may include:

Requesting information about personal data and how it is processed.

Accessing or obtaining a copy of personal data.

Correcting inaccurate or incomplete information.

Requesting deletion or erasure.

Withdrawing consent or objecting to certain processing, including direct marketing.

Requesting restriction of processing or portability where these rights apply.

Opting out of certain targeted advertising, sale or sharing practices where required by applicable law.

Lodging a complaint with a competent privacy regulator or supervisory authority.

To submit a request, contact sales@watsbuy.com and specify your relationship with WatsBuy, the relevant store or account, the request, and a safe way to verify your identity. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. We may request proportionate information to verify the requester and protect personal data. If a merchant controls the data, we may refer your request to that merchant or help route it. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.
