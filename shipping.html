WatsBuy Shipping and Delivery Policy

📅 Effective Date: 26 October 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines the delivery terms for WatsBuy digital software subscriptions, onboarding services, and platform access operated by Ziro Systems Private Limited.

1. Nature of Services and Digital Delivery

WatsBuy is a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and conversational commerce platform. We do not manufacture, warehouse, or physically ship tangible consumer goods for WatsBuy subscription services. All platform access, software modules, merchant dashboards, and API tools are delivered electronically via the Internet.

2. Account Activation and Delivery Timelines

Upon successful registration, verification, and payment processing for a WatsBuy subscription plan:

  • Immediate Electronic Confirmation: Customers receive an immediate order confirmation and receipt via email and/or WhatsApp message.
  • Dashboard Access: Access to standard merchant dashboard features is typically provisioned within minutes of account verification.
  • WhatsApp Business API & Meta Verification: Platform connection to official WhatsApp Business API endpoints depends on Meta account verification and compliance approval, which typically requires 1 to 5 business days, subject to Meta review timelines.
  • Custom Onboarding & Enterprise Implementation: Enterprise setup, custom catalog migration, ERP integrations, and workflow training proceed under an agreed implementation schedule.

3. Access Issues and Technical Support

If you experience any delay, credential issue, or interruption in accessing your WatsBuy merchant dashboard after completing your subscription, contact our support team immediately at sales@watsbuy.com or call +91 8891942727. Our technical support team investigates activation inquiries promptly.

4. Merchant Deliveries to End Customers

Independent merchants using WatsBuy storefronts to sell physical products are solely responsible for publishing their own shipping fees, dispatch schedules, courier partnerships, tracking, and delivery terms. WatsBuy provides order-management and automated messaging software; it does not act as a freight carrier, warehousing provider, or courier for merchant goods.
