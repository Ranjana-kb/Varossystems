Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes. Save 15% with annual billing compared with paying monthly for 12 months. Annual subscriptions are billed upfront.

Do I get both an online storefront and WhatsApp storefront?

Yes. All standard plans include a dual-platform online and WhatsApp storefront with custom domain integration and centralized product, inventory and order management.

Are Meta messaging and payment processing fees included?

No. Applicable Meta charges, payment gateway fees, taxes and optional add-on charges are separate.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Contact the WatsBuy team to discuss plan upgrades and billing adjustments.

ℹ️ Important billing inclusions & Meta fees

  • Platform SaaS Inclusions: All subscription plans include the WhatsApp Store, official WhatsApp Cloud API connectivity, WhatsApp Flows checkout, catalogue, cart and order management, standard payment functionality, bulk broadcasting, Meta-approved messaging workflows, customer management, and analytics.
  • Premium Add-ons & Custom Integrations: Custom ERP integrations, custom workflows, additional message templates, payment gateway integrations, third-party API integrations, and other business-specific customizations are available at additional cost, subject to technical feasibility and applicable provider approvals.
  • Official Meta Conversation Fees: WhatsApp Business messaging fees are determined directly by Meta based on conversation categories (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, and Service) and the recipient country code. Because Meta messaging rates vary across global regions and may be updated periodically by Meta, WatsBuy does not mark up or invent messaging rates. Meta conversation fees are billed directly through your Meta Business Manager payment method or passed through strictly at actual Meta rates.
  • No Hidden Lock-In: Monthly subscriptions can be upgraded or adjusted at any billing cycle. Annual billing provides an upfront 15% discount across all standard tiers.
