1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which payments made for WatsBuy services may be cancelled, refunded, or adjusted.

WatsBuy is a WhatsApp-based commerce and e-commerce Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform operated by Ziro Systems Private Limited, located at Alappat Buildings, No. VII/27-E, Muthuvara, Puzhakkal (P.O), Thrissur, Kerala – 680553, India.

This policy applies to WatsBuy subscriptions, onboarding, setup, and other services purchased directly from WatsBuy. Purchases made from independent merchants using WatsBuy-powered storefronts are subject to the relevant merchant’s policies and applicable law.

2. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of their WatsBuy subscription at any time by contacting our support team or using the cancellation facility available in their account, where provided.

Unless otherwise stated in a written agreement:

  • Cancellation will take effect at the end of the current paid billing period.
  • Access to paid services may continue until that period expires.
  • Cancellation will stop future subscription renewals once processed.
  • Cancelling a subscription does not automatically entitle the customer to a refund for the remaining billing period.

Any cancellation rights that cannot legally be excluded will remain unaffected.

3. Subscription Refunds

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started, including where a customer uses the service for only part of that period.

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payments for the same subscription or invoice.
  • Verified billing errors attributable to WatsBuy.
  • A refund expressly promised under a written offer or agreement.
  • A material failure by WatsBuy to provide a paid service that cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • Any situation in which a refund is required by applicable law.

All other requests will be assessed individually.

4. Setup and Onboarding Fees

Fees for setup, onboarding, configuration, integration, training, custom development, and implementation are generally non-refundable once the relevant work has commenced or been delivered.

If WatsBuy is unable to commence or deliver a paid setup service for reasons within its reasonable control, the customer may contact us for a review. Depending on the circumstances and work already completed, WatsBuy may offer rescheduling, account credit, a partial refund, or another appropriate resolution.

5. Free Trials and Promotional Offers

Free trials, discounts, promotional credits, and special offers are subject to their specific terms and expiry dates.

Unless otherwise stated:

  • Promotional credits are not redeemable for cash.
  • Discounts and promotional benefits are non-transferable.
  • Promotional offers cannot be refunded as cash.
  • Any automatic conversion from a free trial to a paid subscription will be governed by the billing terms disclosed when the customer signs up and applicable law.

6. Charges Generally Not Eligible for Refund

To the extent permitted by law, the following charges are generally non-refundable:

  • Subscription fees for a billing period that has already started.
  • Setup, onboarding, training, implementation, and custom development work already performed.
  • Usage-based fees and messaging charges already incurred.
  • Third-party platform, hosting, integration, and payment-processing fees.
  • Services already used before cancellation or suspension.
  • Fees expressly identified as non-refundable in an applicable written agreement.

This section does not remove any mandatory statutory rights available to customers.

7. Third-Party Platforms and WhatsApp/Meta Charges

WatsBuy may use third-party providers for messaging, payment processing, hosting, integrations, and other functionality.

Charges imposed directly by third parties may be governed by their respective terms. Where a third party retains a fee, WatsBuy cannot guarantee that the amount will be refunded.

WatsBuy does not control Meta or WhatsApp platform approvals, billing decisions, availability, or policy enforcement. Delays or rejections by third-party platforms do not automatically qualify for a refund unless required by law or expressly covered by a written commitment from WatsBuy.

8. Orders Placed with Merchants

If a customer purchases goods or services from an independent merchant through a WatsBuy-powered storefront or conversation, the purchase is generally between the customer and that merchant.

Requests concerning the following should ordinarily be directed to the merchant:

  • Order cancellations.
  • Product returns and exchanges.
  • Damaged, defective, or incorrect products.
  • Missing items or delivery problems.
  • Refunds for returned goods or cancelled orders.

The merchant’s published policy and applicable law will apply. WatsBuy provides technology to support merchant commerce and order management; unless expressly stated otherwise, it is not the seller of the merchant’s products and does not determine the merchant’s refund decisions.

Nothing in this policy limits any statutory rights customers may have against the merchant or another legally responsible party.

9. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for a WatsBuy service, contact:

Email: sales@watsbuy.comSubject: Refund Request

Please include:

  • Your name and registered business or account name.
  • Invoice or transaction reference.
  • Date and amount of payment.
  • Reason for the request.
  • Relevant supporting information or documents.

Do not send passwords, full card numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials.

WatsBuy may request additional information to verify the transaction. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

10. Refund Review and Processing

WatsBuy aims to acknowledge refund requests within five business days and communicate a decision after reviewing the relevant information.

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method where technically possible.

The time required for the funds to appear in the customer’s account depends on the bank, card issuer, payment gateway, or other payment provider. WatsBuy cannot guarantee the processing time of these external providers.

If a refund to the original payment method is not possible, WatsBuy will contact the customer regarding an appropriate alternative, subject to applicable law.

11. International Payments, Currency and Taxes

Refunds will normally be processed in the currency of the original transaction.

Exchange-rate fluctuations, intermediary bank charges, and foreign transaction fees may affect the amount received. Such charges may not be recoverable from WatsBuy.

Taxes will be refunded or adjusted where required under applicable tax laws.

Customers outside India may have additional rights under the mandatory consumer protection laws applicable in their country. Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict rights that cannot legally be waived.

12. Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

If a customer believes a payment is incorrect, we encourage them to contact WatsBuy first so that we can investigate and attempt to resolve the matter.

Customers may also have the right to raise a dispute with their bank or payment provider. Where necessary, WatsBuy may provide transaction and service records to the relevant payment provider to respond to a payment dispute.

13. Changes to This Policy

WatsBuy may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website with its updated date.

Changes will apply prospectively, subject to applicable law and any separate written agreement with the customer.
