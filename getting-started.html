ONBOARDING ROADMAP

Your 5-Step Journey to
Launching Your WhatsApp Store with WatsBuy.

From Meta verification to your first customer order, WatsBuy simplifies the entire onboarding process with structured implementation and technical support.

Browse Help Centre FAQs → View Pricing Plans
01
Step One

Meta WhatsApp API Verification

Connect Your Business to the Official WhatsApp Platform.

Activate your business WhatsApp number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API to enable secure, compliant, and interactive customer communication.

  • Register a new or eligible existing WhatsApp Business number.
  • Complete Meta Business Portfolio setup and required business verification.
  • Configure your business display name, profile, logo, and business information.
  • Activate WhatsApp Cloud API connectivity with WatsBuy.
02
Step Two

Design WhatsApp Flows & Approve Templates

Create Your Customer Shopping Experience.

Configure interactive WhatsApp shopping journeys and Meta-approved messaging templates to deliver a seamless customer experience.

  • Design shopping flows for product discovery, checkout, and order confirmation.
  • Configure My Account, My Orders, Offers, Support, and Contact Us menus.
  • Create and submit WhatsApp message templates for Meta approval.
  • Set up automated order notifications, promotional broadcasts, and customer engagement workflows.
03
Step Three

Product Catalogue Upload & Store Configuration

Bring Your Products to WhatsApp.

Set up your digital storefront with product information, categories, pricing, and inventory through the WatsBuy merchant dashboard.

  • Upload products using Excel/CSV templates or manual entry.
  • Configure product images, descriptions, pricing, and variants.
  • Organize product categories and collections.
  • Configure inventory, order management, and store settings.
04
Step Four

Third-Party API & Payment Integrations

Connect Your Essential Business Services.

Integrate supported payment gateways, delivery services, and business applications to streamline order processing and fulfilment.

  • Payment Gateways: Connect supported payment providers for digital transactions.
  • Delivery Partners: Integrate supported logistics and shipment tracking services.
  • Email & Notifications: Configure email services and business alerts.
  • Custom API Integrations: Connect compatible third-party applications based on business requirements.

Third-party integrations may require additional charges, provider approvals, and technical configuration.

05
Step Five

Go-to-Market & Store Launch

Your WhatsApp Store Is Ready for Business.

Complete final testing, activate your storefront, and start reaching customers through WhatsApp.

  • Test the complete customer journey, from product discovery to checkout and order confirmation.
  • Verify payment functionality, order notifications, and customer support workflows.
  • Launch your WhatsApp store and share your business number or QR code.
  • Start Meta-approved promotional broadcasts to opted-in customers.
  • Monitor orders, customer engagement, and campaign performance through your WatsBuy dashboard.
