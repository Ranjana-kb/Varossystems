Meta Business Verification is Meta's official process to confirm that a Meta Business Portfolio belongs to a legally registered organization. Completing verification increases your daily messaging limits (moving from the unverified 250 conversations/24h tier to Tier 1 [1,000], Tier 2 [10,000], Tier 3 [100,000], and unlimited daily conversations), protects your phone number from automated spam filtering, and is a strict prerequisite for requesting the official Meta Green Tick verified badge.

Category: Meta Business Verification & Compliance

Meta requires government-issued documents that clearly show your exact legal business name, physical operating address, and official business phone number. In India, Meta accepts GST Registration Certificates, Certificates of Incorporation, Udyam Aadhar, or recent utility bills/bank statements matching the registered business name. In the UAE and GCC, Meta accepts Trade Licenses and Commercial Register certificates. Ensure documents are clear, unaltered, and up-to-date.

Category: Meta Business Verification & Compliance

Meta typically reviews business verification submissions within 1 to 5 business days. You can monitor review status in real-time inside your Meta Business Manager under Settings > Security Centre. While WatsBuy provides complete guidance and document review to ensure your submission is accurate, review times and approval decisions are strictly governed by Meta.

Category: Meta Business Verification & Compliance

If Meta requests additional information or rejects your verification, check for subtle discrepancies between your Meta Business Portfolio details and your legal documents (such as abbreviations, suite numbers, or different phone numbers). Ensure your website is live with an active domain email (e.g. name@yourcompany.com) rather than a generic Gmail or Yahoo address. You can update details and re-submit directly in Meta Business Manager.

Category: Meta Business Verification & Compliance

The Green Tick (Official Business Account badge) is granted directly by Meta to verified brands with proven authenticity and high public search interest/notability. Eligibility requires: 1) Completed Meta Business Verification, 2) Two-Factor Authentication enabled on your Business Manager, 3) Tier 2 or higher messaging limits, and 4) Organic news coverage or high brand recognition. Meta reviews badge requests independently without charging fees.
