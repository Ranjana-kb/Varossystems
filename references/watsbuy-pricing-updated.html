Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI included in every plan?

AI-powered features are available as optional paid add-ons across WatsBuy subscription plans. AI shopping assistants, voice commerce, visual product discovery, intelligent recommendations and automated AI workflows are priced based on selected features, usage and integration requirements.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes. Save 15% with annual billing compared with paying monthly for 12 months. Annual subscriptions are billed upfront. The displayed annual prices are rounded to the nearest ₹10.

Do I get both an online storefront and WhatsApp storefront?

Yes. All standard plans include a dual-platform online and WhatsApp storefront with custom domain integration and centralized product, inventory and order management.

Are Meta messaging and payment processing fees included?

No. Applicable Meta WhatsApp messaging charges, payment gateway fees, taxes and optional add-on charges are separate.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Contact the WatsBuy team to discuss plan upgrades and billing adjustments.
