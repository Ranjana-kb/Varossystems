WatsBuy website prototype
========================

Open index.html in a modern browser to preview the website.

Included:
- Responsive one-page website prototype
- Navigation and dropdowns- Navigation with dropdowns — clicking any nav tag or link navigates to its
  respective section (anchor-based in-page navigation)
- Website vs. WhatsApp commerce comparison tabs
- Illustrative funnel
- Interactive revenue recovery calculator
- Searchable FAQ with all 58 questions from FAQ.docx
- Pricing, features, solutions, reseller, enterprise and contact sections
- Policy page placeholders

Important before publishing:
1. Replace the CSS text-based logo mark with the official logo asset if exact logo fidelity is required.
2. Connect the contact form to a secure backend/CRM; this prototype does not transmit submissions.
3. Verify plan entitlements, prices and company contact details.
4. Replace policy placeholders with approved legal text.
5. Configure real application/login destinations.
6. Demo metrics are illustrative and must not be represented as actual customer results.
7. Review third-party benchmark citations and current methodology before publishing.

This is a static front-end prototype, not a production-connected SaaS application.
