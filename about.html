About Us

WatsBuy is redefining how businesses connect with customers, showcase products, and convert conversations into sales. We are a conversational commerce and sales automation platform that helps businesses move beyond traditional websites and fragmented shopping experiences to a more direct, conversational, and effective way of selling.

1. Our Story

Traditional e-commerce often depends on customers visiting a website, searching through products, navigating multiple pages, and completing several checkout steps before placing an order. Along the way, businesses can lose valuable sales opportunities.

In a world where customers spend more time messaging, browsing social media, and interacting through digital channels, businesses need more than an online store. They need a smarter way to reach customers, engage them at the right moment, simplify purchasing, and build lasting relationships. That is where WatsBuy comes in.

WatsBuy was built around a simple idea: Why wait for customers to visit your store when you can bring your store, products, and shopping experience directly to them?

By combining WhatsApp commerce, direct product discovery, native checkout flows, and sales automation, WatsBuy helps businesses create a more connected customer journey—from product discovery to purchase and beyond.

2. What We Do

WatsBuy brings essential commerce, customer engagement, and sales automation capabilities together in one platform—helping businesses reach customers, simplify shopping, and turn conversations into sales.

  • WhatsApp Commerce: Showcase products, share catalogues, engage shoppers, and enable customers to browse products, add to cart, and place orders through conversational shopping experiences.
  • Automated Sales Engagement: Automate customer enquiries, personalised follow-ups, promotional journeys, abandoned-cart recovery, and repeat-purchase campaigns to improve customer engagement and sales opportunities.
  • Bulk WhatsApp Broadcasting & Campaigns: Reach large customer segments with targeted promotional messages, product carousels, new-arrival announcements, special offers, seasonal campaigns, and personalised updates. Use customer segmentation and campaign management to deliver relevant messages at scale, subject to WhatsApp's messaging policies, approved templates, and applicable consent requirements.
  • Officially Approved WhatsApp Flows & Message Templates: Create structured, interactive customer journeys using WhatsApp Flows and approved message templates through supported official WhatsApp Business Platform integrations. Simplify product enquiries, customer information collection, order updates, appointment requests, feedback, and other business interactions while maintaining compliance with Meta's applicable approval and messaging requirements.
  • Simplified Ordering & Payments: Connect product selection, cart management, order processing, payment links, and supported payment gateways into a smoother purchasing journey—from product discovery to checkout and order fulfilment.
  • Customer Insights & Analytics: Understand customer engagement, monitor campaign performance, identify abandoned sales opportunities, and gain actionable insights to improve conversions, customer retention, and business growth.

3. Why WatsBuy?

We believe the future of commerce is not just about having an online store. It is about making shopping easier, conversations more meaningful, and customer engagement more effective. WatsBuy helps businesses:

  • Reach customers where they already communicate.
  • Reduce friction in product discovery and purchasing.
  • Respond faster with automated customer engagement.
  • Automate repetitive sales and support activities.
  • Build stronger customer relationships through personalised interactions.
  • Bring product discovery, conversations, and ordering closer together.
